QUICK ANSWERS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What to expect when exploring a digital product or software project with Vashodiya Labs.

What kinds of projects can Vashodiya Labs help with?

We work on websites and web apps, mobile apps, AI and workflow automation, software integrations, cloud and DevOps, and UI/UX design. Share your goal and we can discuss the right scope.

How does a project get started?

Send an enquiry with your goals, users, and any requirements you already know. We’ll review the details and follow up to clarify scope and possible next steps.

How do you estimate project cost and timeline?

Cost and timing depend on the scope, integrations, and delivery needs. After understanding the requirements, we can discuss an estimate and suitable milestones.

Can you improve an existing website or software product?

Yes. Tell us what you have, what is working, and what needs to change. We can discuss improvements, integrations, redesign, or maintenance based on the project.

Can you help after launch?

Post-launch improvements and website maintenance can be discussed as part of the project scope, including updates, monitoring, and ongoing product development.

Where is Vashodiya Labs based, and do you work remotely?

We’re based in Amritsar, Punjab, and work with teams in other locations. Use the project enquiry form to tell us where you’re based and what you need.
