Privacy Policy

Initial draft — review and update this policy to reflect the services, data practices and legal requirements that apply before publication.

Vashodiya Labs collects the information you submit through our website forms to respond to project inquiries and provide our services.

Information we collect

  • Name, email address, phone number and company name where provided.
  • Project description, service selection and relevant timeline or budget preferences.
  • When an invoice is issued, client billing name, email, billing address, optional GSTIN, service description, invoice amount and payment status.
  • Technical or usage information automatically collected by the server, such as IP address and browser metadata.
  • The website page path where an inquiry was submitted, to help us understand which services visitors ask about.

How we use it

We use the information to understand your requirements, respond to your inquiry, issue and collect payment for invoices, maintain accounting records, manage the project lifecycle and communicate essential service updates. We do not sell personal data.

Card details are entered on Stripe's hosted checkout page and are not stored by Vashodiya Labs. Stripe processes payment details under its own privacy terms.

Optional website analytics

If Google Analytics is configured, it is loaded only after you choose to allow analytics in the consent prompt. You can decline analytics and still use the website and inquiry form. You can clear this choice by clearing this site's browser storage.

Data retention

Inquiry and invoice records may be retained for applicable tax, accounting, business communication and compliance periods. Follow-up notes and invoice links are stored in the protected business database.

Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect submitted information. Please do not send confidential data through public forms before a secure agreement is in place.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us through the main inquiry form or via the project discussion channel established with our team.
